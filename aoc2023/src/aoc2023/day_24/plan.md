  A ---va---->       B --vb-->
  |--------(b-a)-----|

  At time t1 the rock with velocity r catches up with A.

  A position: a + t * va
  B position: b + t * vb

  The distance between the snowflakes is: (b - a) + t * (vb - va)
  Every further second, the rock moves r while B moves vb so it catches up by `(r-vb)`

  Rock will hit B _if_ `(b-a) + t(vb-va) = t2(r-vb)`
